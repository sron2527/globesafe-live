ABOUT GLOBESAFE LIVE

Public information, clearly sourced.

GlobeSafe Live is designed as a public-access situational awareness tool. No account is required.

01

About

We combine public hazard feeds, public media indexes, authorized camera directories and public internet radio into a simple global dashboard.

02

Data Sources

Earthquakes: USGS. Natural events: NASA EONET. Conflict watch: GDELT media index in the MVP, with an ACLED connector planned for verified event mapping. Radio: Radio Browser. Base map: OpenFreeMap using OpenStreetMap data.

03

Privacy

The MVP does not require an account. Version 2 stores watch areas, favorite countries and favorite radio stations in your browser local storage. Browser notifications require explicit permission and can be disabled in browser settings.

04

Disclaimer

This service is informational and may be delayed, incomplete, duplicated or inaccurate. Always follow official local authorities and emergency services for urgent safety decisions.

Advertising & editorial separation

Advertising areas are visually separated from alerts, map controls, emergency information and radio controls. Sponsored content must never be styled as an emergency notice or official safety instruction.

Conflict information

War and conflict reporting can be incomplete, disputed or rapidly changing. GlobeSafe Live labels media-derived reports and does not present them as verified military intelligence, evacuation orders or predictions.
